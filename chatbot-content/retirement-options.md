## 403(b) compliance support

403(b) Compliance Regulations

The Internal Revenue Service issued regulations on 403(b) plans that generally became effective on January 1, 2009. These regulations impact all 403(b) plans and require a written plan document that contains the material plan provisions. If AGFinancial is your exclusive service provider, compliance implementation can be easier. Compliance may be more complex if you use multiple providers.

403(b) Guidelines of Administrative Responsibilities

AGFinancial and MBA provide multiple administrative functions for compliance with regulations; however, some administrative functions remain your responsibility as the employer fiduciary. Download the checklist that matches your organization type.

This information is not legal or tax advice. Work with your ministry’s legal and tax advisors to ensure you have satisfied all IRS 403(b) regulation requirements.

For assistance, contact 800.622.7526 or email 403bregs@agfinancial.org.

## Online contributions

Manage Contributions

Minimize monthly data entry, and maximize accuracy as you make retirement contributions on behalf of your employees.

### Set-up is easy

- Create a new user account for your company in Online Access.
- Select “403(b) Employer” as the Account Type.
- Contact Client Services at 866.621.1787 or clientservices@agfinancial.org for your Employer Code to complete your account setup.
- When your account is established, you may remit funds by EFT or check.

### Public setup steps

1. Create a new user account for your company.
2. Select “403(b) Employer” as the Account Type.
3. Get your Employer Code.
